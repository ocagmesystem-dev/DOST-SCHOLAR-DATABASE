Scholarship grant
Expected completion is worked out automatically from the date started. The scholarship covers 2 school years, counting only months with official classes (August to May; June and July are not counted), which is 20 class months. Lateral entrants are capped at the end of the 2nd school year of the program, so they get fewer months if they start mid-year. Full time and part time currently count the same. You can still change the expected completion, e.g. for an extension.
